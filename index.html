    How to play

    You are black. White has a forced mate, and the game value says how long you can put it off: ω4 at the start, counting down to 0 at mate. Every limit you reach is a number you get to choose.

    Tap a piece to light the squares it can reach. Tap one to move. A ray that never ends asks you for a number instead. Drag the board to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use the links under the value to jump between the throne room, the towers, the cannons and the wings.

    When white must reply to what you just did, the value says so. Those are the moves that cost white nothing on the main line, which is why they are worth making.
