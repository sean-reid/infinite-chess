    How to play

    You are black. White has a forced mate, and the game value says how long you can put it off: ω4 at the start, counting down to 0 at mate. Every limit you reach is a number you get to choose.

    Tap a piece to light the squares it can reach, then tap one to move. A ray that never ends also offers a number: how far along it to go, with buttons for the landings that matter, such as the wings a cannon shot can reach. The hint fills in its own choice and rings the square it means. Drag the board to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use the Go to buttons for the throne room, the tower, cannon and wing in play, or the last move.

    After white replies, the line under the value says why it moved. Replies that read as answering a gate or shoring up a cannon were forced, and those moves cost you nothing on the main line, which is why they are worth making. The hint names the next one.
